    Email sending setup (EmailJS, free)
    1. Create a free account at emailjs.com (200 emails a month).
    2. Under Email Services, connect your Gmail or Outlook and copy the Service ID.
    3. Under Email Templates, create a template. Set To Email to {{to_email}}, Subject to {{subject}}, and the body to {{message}}. Copy the Template ID.
    4. Under Account, copy your Public Key, paste all three below, and send a test.
